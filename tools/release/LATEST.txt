[en]
- New effect, Distance Depth of Field (PC Settings, Effects): the far distance goes softly out of focus.
- Tanks: their tracks roll as they drive and their links are no longer stretched; the T-34's periscope frame no longer smears.
- Tank tracks in the snow: a real tread pattern pressed in, sunk into the ground and lit, no longer dark bands.
- Level 30: the road is whole, its edge a smooth line, with no polygons of snow cut into it or seams between its tiles.
- Branches passing right in front of the camera (a tank's periscope by the roadside firs) no longer cover the screen.
- Planes' shadows on the ground move smoothly.
